In the matter of: what we do

Every service, handled like evidence

From the first attempt to the final affidavit, every job is documented, time-stamped, and returned ready to file. One office, eight ways to move your case forward.

Process Service

Summons, complaints, subpoenas, evictions, postings, garnishments and citations, served personally, promptly, and in full compliance with Georgia law.

Private Investigation

Discreet investigative work supporting attorneys, businesses, and individuals. Backgrounds, due diligence, and case support.

Document Retrieval

Court records, filings, and documents pulled from clerks' offices across Georgia and delivered to your inbox or your door.

Mobile Notary

Commissioned notary services that come to you. Closings, affidavits, and sworn statements, wherever you need them signed.

SkipTrace

Evasive or missing? We locate hard-to-find individuals using investigative databases and old-fashioned fieldwork, then complete the serve.

Courier

Same-day document runs between your office, the courthouse, and opposing counsel, so deadlines never depend on the mail.

Surveillance

Discreet, professional surveillance with detailed reporting, because sometimes the facts have to be seen to be proven.

Apostille & Authentication

Need documents authenticated? We make the process simple. Apostille and document authentication assistance. Fast. Secure. Professional.
